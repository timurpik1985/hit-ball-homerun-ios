❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap and swipe upward to flick the ball. Time it well to launch the longest home runs! Try to hit the ball at the perfect angle and power.

How do I unlock special balls?

Special balls (Silver, Gold, Diamond) unlock by:

  • 🎬 Watching optional rewarded video ads
  • 📏 Reaching distance milestones

Other balls (Fire, Ice, Ghost, etc.) unlock through gameplay progression!

What are the rings during flight?

Rings appear during the ball's flight - fly through them for bonus distance! The more rings you hit, the further your ball travels.

What about the fruits and bombs?

While the ball flies, you can swipe to slash fruits for distance bonuses! Watch out for bombs - they reduce your distance!

How does the leaderboard work?

Save your score in Hall of Fame with your nickname. Your rank is shown at the top so you can always find yourself, even with thousands of players!

Why are there ads?

The game is completely FREE thanks to family-friendly ads:

  • ✅ Play the entire game without watching ads
  • ✅ Watch optional ads to unlock special balls
  • ✅ Choose to allow or deny ad tracking

Is this game safe for kids?

Yes! Hit Ball Homerun is rated 4+ with:

  • ✅ No violence or scary content
  • ✅ No chat or social features
  • ✅ Family-friendly ads only
  • ✅ No personal information collected
  • ✅ COPPA compliant

How do I disable ad tracking?

Go to your iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or revoke permission for our app.

How do I reset my progress?

To reset your game progress, simply delete and reinstall the app. All progress is stored locally on your device.
